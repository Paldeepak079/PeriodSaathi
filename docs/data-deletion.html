Data & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data in Period Saathi. Below are the steps to delete your account and all associated data.

Delete Your Account In-App

1
Open Period Saathi and go to the Settings tab.
2
Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the settings screen.
3
Confirm your deletion. Your account and all cloud data will be permanently removed.

Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to:

Email: periodsaathi@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your registered email address or Google account
  • The word "DELETE" in the subject line

We will process your request within 30 days.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Supabase account and all cloud-synced data
  • Period cycle history and predictions
  • Symptom and mood logs
  • Community posts and comments (de-anonymized)
  • App preferences and settings
  • Payment history references

Retained: Anonymized analytics data (no personal identifiers) may be retained in Firebase for up to 90 days per Google's data retention policy.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

If you use the app as a guest (without signing in), all data is stored only on your device. To delete it:

  • Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Period Saathi > Storage
  • Tap "Clear Data" or "Uninstall"
