### Full Description (up to 4000 chars)

Period Saathi is your empathetic cycle companion — a beautiful, privacy-first period tracking app designed to make cycle tracking feel like self-care, not a chore. Track periods, log symptoms, discover patterns, and earn rewards — all with a cute mascot by your side.

**✨ Features**

🌸 **Smart Period & Ovulation Predictions**
Log your period and let Saathi predict your next cycle, fertile window, and ovulation day with increasing accuracy over time.

📅 **Beautiful Calendar View**
Color-coded calendar shows period days, fertile window, predicted dates, and logged symptoms at a glance. Toggle between "Planning Baby" and "Avoiding" goals.

💧 **Quick Logging**
Log water intake, meals, medicine, flow level, and more with one tap. The faster you log, the better your insights.

🌙 **Sleep & Wellness Tracking**
Track sleep hours, heart rate (BPM), exercise, and mood. View trends over each cycle phase.

🧘 **Cramp Relief Yoga Flow**
Guided yoga sequences designed specifically for menstrual cramp relief. Includes child's pose, cat-cow, butterfly pose, and more.

🌿 **Wellness Remedies**
Personalized remedies for cramps, headaches, bloating, fatigue, and mood swings based on your cycle phase.

❤️ **Partner Mode (Optional)**
Share cycle insights with your partner so they can support you better. Fully opt-in and user-controlled.

🔒 **Privacy First — Everything is Local**
All your health data is stored securely on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no data sharing. You are in complete control.

🔐 **Biometric Lock / Stealth Mode**
Lock the app behind fingerprint or face unlock. Enable Stealth Mode to disguise the app icon and require PIN access.

🎁 **Rewards & Challenges**
Stay consistent with logging streaks. Earn badges for milestones like "7-day streak", "Wellness Warrior", and more.

📓 **Cycle Journal & Mood Map**
Write private journal entries and track how your mood changes throughout your cycle. Beautiful mood map visualization.

⏳ **Time Capsule**
Write letters to your future self about each cycle. Open them months later and see how far you've come.

👗 **Saathi's Wardrobe**
Unlock outfits and accessories for your Saathi mascot by tracking consistently. Make your companion uniquely yours.

🌐 **Bilingual Support**
Supports English and Hindi, making it accessible to millions of users across India.

**Why Period Saathi?**

- ✅ **100% Free** — No subscriptions, no premium tiers
- ✅ **No Account Required** — Download and start tracking immediately
- ✅ **No Internet Needed** — Works fully offline
- ✅ **No Ads** — Your health data should never be interrupted by ads
- ✅ **Open Design** — Beautiful Material 3 glassmorphism design

**Permissions Explained**

- 📷 **Body Sensors (Wear OS)** — Only if you connect a Wear OS device. Never used otherwise.
- 📱 **Notifications** — Opt-in reminders for logging, period predictions, and wellness tips.
- ✉️ **SMS** — Only when you explicitly choose to share cycle info with your partner. User-initiated only.

**Content Rating:** Everyone (13+ recommended for health content)

**Note:** Period Saathi is a tracking and educational tool. It does not provide medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.
