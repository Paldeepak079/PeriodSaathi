Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Period Saathi is designed with privacy by default. We believe your menstrual health data is deeply personal and should remain under your complete control. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Period Saathi operates entirely on your device. We do not have servers, databases, or analytics services. We do not track you, profile you, or sell your data.

What is stored on your device:

  • Cycle start dates and length
  • Symptoms and flow level logs
  • Mood entries and journal notes
  • Wellness scores and health metrics
  • App preferences (theme, language, notification settings)
  • Stealth mode PIN (stored as a secure hash)

All of the above is stored locally using Android's built-in encrypted storage (Room database and DataStore).

2. Internet & Network Access

Period Saathi does not require internet access to function. The app works completely offline. No data is ever transmitted over the network unless you explicitly choose to:

  • SMS Sharing (Optional): If you use the Partner Mode feature to share cycle information via SMS, the app uses your device's SMS capability. The content is composed by you and sent through your carrier's standard SMS. Period Saathi does not see or store the message content.
  • Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, this is handled entirely by Google's authentication SDK. Period Saathi receives only a basic profile token and does not store it.

3. Third-Party Services

Period Saathi integrates the following third-party SDKs, all of which operate locally on your device:

  • AndroidX Biometric — For device biometric authentication (fingerprint, face unlock). No biometric data is stored by Period Saathi.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional authentication. Handled by Google's SDK per their privacy policy.

No third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are used.

4. Data Security

Your data is protected by Android's built-in sandboxing. Other apps cannot access Period Saathi's data. If you enable Stealth Mode, the app is additionally protected by biometric authentication or a PIN (your PIN is stored as a SHA-256 hash, not in plain text).

5. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time:

  • Clear app data: Android Settings → Apps → Period Saathi → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all associated data

6. Children's Privacy

Period Saathi is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app and on this page. Your continued use of Period Saathi after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
